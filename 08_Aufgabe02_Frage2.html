What does Globex Toys actually need here?

Translated by Sherlock

Subject: Progress report before choosing a bank

Hello,

To be honest, we are a little concerned about Mr Moratti's proposal from yesterday. He seems to believe that one system will solve all problems. However, international business is not so simple.

We're expanding in several directions at once. Canada and the UK keep growing as export markets - Canada especially, faster than anything else we do. We've also just started sourcing electronics components for our Educational Computer Games line from a new supplier in Vietnam, and we're in early talks with a logistics partner in Poland, though that's still early days.

As a customer, we want to have one clear point of contact for our day-to-day questions. But when something really matters, we still want to speak to someone who actually knows our business - not a form, not a queue, not a system that decides on our behalf. If that's what Mr Moratti's "one system" means, it's the wrong direction for us.

The Canadian dollar has become our largest currency exposure by some distance, and it's still growing. We don't want to call someone every time the rate moves - we'd rather manage our FX positions ourselves, flexibly, whenever it suits us, online.

Our new supplier in Vietnam has already caused two shipment delays this quarter. Nothing dramatic yet, but with the volumes we're planning for next year, we need real support on the documentary side of international trade - the kind of protection that comes with proper letters of credit and collections, not just a phone number for when something goes wrong.

And then there's the elephant in the room: the Board approved our capacity expansion for Outdoor Play Equipment back in the spring, but we still haven't signed off on how to finance it. Before we commit, we want to actually see the credit lines, the collateral requirements, the whole process - not sign something we only understand once it's too late to change our minds.

We are currently reviewing our banking relationships, and this is exactly the moment where a mistake would cost us. We'd rather take our time and get it right than let someone else - man or machine - decide for us.

Best regards,
Leonie Schneider
Head of Finance & Strategy, Globex Toys
